About Our Club

Welcome to the BVHS chess club! We meet weekly to play, learn, and enjoy chess together.

Welcome to our new Bow Valley School Chess Club! Starting in 2026, we invite students of all experience levels – from total beginners to seasoned players – to join us. Our goal is simple: have fun and enjoy the game together! We believe that while winning feels great, what matters most is kindness and sportsmanship. We proudly uphold our BHS motto: Respect, Responsibility, and Resilience. This means we treat each other with kindness, show up ready to participate (and help keep our equipment safe!), and practice resilience by staying positive even when a game gets tough. Stay tuned for updates— we are looking into hosting at the Cochrane Lions Club soon, where there may even be snacks!
