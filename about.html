About Our Club

Welcome to our new Bow Valley School Chess Club! Starting in 2026, we invite students of all experience levels—from total beginners to seasoned players—to join us. Our goal is simple: have fun and enjoy the game together!

We believe that while winning feels great, what matters most is kindness and sportsmanship. We are working hard behind the scenes right now to build a space where every student feels welcomed, supported, and inspired to play.

We proudly uphold our BHS motto: Respect, Responsibility, and Resilience. This means we treat each other with kindness, show up ready to participate while taking responsibility for our gear—respecting the pieces and ensuring we "leave no trace"—and practicing resilience by staying positive even when a game gets tough.

Stay tuned for updates—we are currently scouting locations like the Cochrane Lions Club and preparing everything needed to make this club a success! This vision is being brought to life by a dedicated team of students who are handling every detail from web development to logistics.
